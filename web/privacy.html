CEEKAY Volunteer Portal — Privacy Notice

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This notice explains how information submitted through the CEEKAY volunteer expression-of-interest portal is handled.

Information collected

We collect your name, phone number, optional email and community, chosen LGA, ward and polling unit, age-eligibility confirmation, election-day and training availability, prior experience, smartphone access, preferred contact method, and consent confirmation. The system also records application status and submission time.

Why information is used

Authorized volunteer coordinators use this information to review expressions of interest, contact applicants, plan training and coordinate volunteer activities. Applying does not guarantee selection, appointment or INEC accreditation.

Access and sharing

Volunteer application records are available through the restricted administrator interface. We do not intend to publish applicants' personal details on the public portal. Information may be handled by the technical providers used to host the portal and database. We do not claim this system is operated by INEC.

Political involvement

This is a political volunteer portal associated with Hon. Ceekay Igara and the Labour Party in Abia State. By submitting an application, you understand that your expression of interest may reveal a political association. Please do not submit anyone else's personal information without their permission.

Retention and deletion

Application records are retained for volunteer coordination and related administrative needs. A definitive retention period is being established; until it is published, you can contact the team to request access, correction or deletion. Requests will be reviewed, including any applicable legal obligations.

Contact and your choices
